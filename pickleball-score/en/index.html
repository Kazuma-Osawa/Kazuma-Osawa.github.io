Pickle Caller

Pickleball score calls and group pairings

日本語

Pickle Caller keeps track of the score and the serving order in pickleball doubles. Just tap the team that won each rally. The app shows the call (such as “4-2-1”), reads it aloud, and shows on a court diagram who serves next and from which side.

How to use

  1. Tap “New Match” at the top right to choose the score you play to (11, 15 or 21) and the team that serves first. You can also change the team names and the voice language (English, Japanese, Korean or Chinese) there.
  2. After each rally, tap the big button of the team that won it. The orange button at the bottom is your team; the blue one at the top is your opponents.
  3. The call and what it means (serving team’s score, receiving team’s score, and which server is serving) appear in the middle. On the court diagram, the player glowing yellow serves and the player at the end of the arrow receives.
  4. If you tap the wrong button, use “Undo” at the top left as many times as you need.
  5. When a team wins, the match is saved to your match history.

Pairings

Open it with the people icon at the top. Enter the number of players (4–16), courts (1–4) and rounds, then tap “Create Pairings” to get doubles pairs and a match order.

  • Players take turns sitting out, and the app avoids repeating partners and opponents where possible.
  • Tap the winning pair to add the result to the “Standings”. Tap again to undo it.
  • You can add rounds later. If you leave names blank, players are shown by number.

FAQ

What do the three numbers in the call mean?
In order: the serving team’s score, the receiving team’s score, and which player on the serving team is serving (1 or 2). A game starts at “0-0-2” because the first serving team gets only one server at the start.
Does it support singles or rally scoring (where either team can score)?
Currently it supports doubles with side-out scoring only (only the serving team can score).
How do I turn off the voice?
Tap the speaker icon at the top to turn reading aloud on or off. Tap the call to hear it again.
How do I delete match history?
Open the history with the clipboard icon at the top, then swipe a match to the left to delete it. You can also use “Delete All”.
Are there ads during a match?
No ads appear on the scoring screen. Banner ads appear at the bottom of the match history and pairings screens. A full-screen ad may sometimes appear when you start a new match after finishing one (never during your first three matches).
How do I change my ad settings?
In regions where consent is required for ads (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), use “Privacy Settings” on the New Match screen. You can change tracking permission at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking. Every feature works even if you don’t allow tracking.
Can I move my data to a new iPhone?
Match history and pairings are stored only on your iPhone. They carry over if you back up and restore your whole iPhone with iCloud or a computer. Deleting the app erases them permanently.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Scoring, reading aloud, history and pairings all work without a connection. Ads are not shown while offline.
