Privacy Policy

Pickle Caller

日本語

This policy explains how Kazuma Osawa (“the developer”) handles user information in the iPhone app Pickle Caller (Japanese name: ピックル点数係; “the app”). If this English version and the Japanese version differ, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Information the app stores on your device

The app stores the following information only on your iPhone:

  • Settings (score to play to, voice on/off and language, team names, etc.)
  • Match history (date, team names, scores)
  • Pairings (names you entered, pairs and match order, results)

This information is never sent to the developer or any third party. You can delete history within the app, and deleting the app erases everything. If iPhone backup (iCloud or a computer) is turned on, this information may be included in your backups.

Calls are read aloud with the iPhone’s built-in speech feature, entirely on the device. No audio or text is sent anywhere.

2. Advertising

The app uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To show ads and measure their performance, AdMob may collect information such as:

  • Device identifiers such as the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Usage data such as ad views and taps
  • Diagnostic data about how the app runs

Names and other information you enter in match history or pairings are never used for ads or passed to AdMob.

You can choose whether to allow tracking on the prompt shown at first launch, and change it at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security > Tracking. Every feature of the app works even if you don’t allow tracking.

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent for ads at first launch. You can change your choices with “Privacy Settings” on the New Match screen.

For how Google handles information, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not receive any user information and therefore does not share it with third parties. Information collected by AdMob for ads is handled under Google’s policies above.

4. Children

The app is not primarily directed at children under 13.

5. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version and its effective date will be posted on this page.

6. Contact
